Why your layout should start with a grid

SO
Sam Okafor
8 Oct 2026 · 6 min read

A grid is a set of invisible lines that everything on the page lines up with. It sounds dull, and it is the reason good pages feel calm.

When every element follows the same columns and spacing, readers spot patterns quickly. Headings, images and text blocks feel related, because they are. You also make fewer decisions, since the grid has already made them for you.

Start with columns

Twelve columns divide neatly into halves, thirds and quarters. That is why Bootstrap uses them. On a phone the columns stack, and on a desktop they sit side by side.

Good layout is mostly about deciding what lines up with what.

Sam Okafor

Keep spacing consistent

Pick a small set of spacing sizes and reuse them. Bootstrap's spacing utilities, such as mb-3 and py-5, make this easy without writing extra CSS.

Check on a real phone

Resize your browser, then pick up an actual phone. Line length, tap targets and image sizes all look different in your hand.
